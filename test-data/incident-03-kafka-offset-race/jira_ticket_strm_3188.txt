Title: Scale Kafka consumer throughput for Q2 traffic growth
Priority: P3 - Medium
Status: DONE

Reporter: Infrastructure - Yuna Park

Closed: 2025-04-01
Labels: kafka, performance, scaling, config-change

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Traffic analysis shows our Kafka consumer lag has been growing over the past 6 weeks,
particularly for the streamflow-events-cg consumer group. At peak hours the lag is reaching
40,000+ messages, causing downstream processing delays of up to 8 minutes.

Proposed changes:
  1. Double the Kafka topic partition count from 16 to 32 (enables more parallelism)
  2. Increase MaxPollRecords from 50 to 500 (reduce poll overhead per consumer thread)

These changes together should increase consumer throughput by approximately 8-10x.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
--------------------
2025-03-20 - Marco Delgado:
Partition doubling completed in staging. Consumer rebalancing completed cleanly. No
events dropped during rebalance. Lag cleared within 4 minutes of applying changes.

2025-04-01 - Marco Delgado:
Deployed to production during maintenance window (02:00-03:00 UTC).
  - Partition count: 16 → 32 (applied)
  - MaxPollRecords: 50 → 500 (applied via consumer group config update)
  - Consumer group rebalance completed successfully
  - Lag metric improved from 40,000 to < 5,000 within 10 minutes

Closing ticket as successfully deployed.

TESTING NOTES
-------------
Staging load test ran for 2 hours at 2x normal peak. No issues observed.
Note: Staging test did not include concurrent consumer thread scenarios with partition
rebalances under high MaxPollRecords. Test data used idempotent event types only.

RISK NOTES
----------
Low risk. Partition increase is additive. MaxPollRecords increase improves throughput.
No consumer application code changes required.
